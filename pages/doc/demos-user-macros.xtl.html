programdemos/user-macros.xtl

A macro library of your own: userlibs/Repeat.xtlm (in the live demo, Repeat.xtlm among your files). It is found as a library of your own is, and its macro r:t_imes< writes statements as many times as you ask, when the program is expanded: a loop unrolled before it runs.

Run it with ./demos/user-macros.xtl or "xetal run demos/user-macros.xtl". It prints hip hip three times, hooray, then 32. "xetal expand demos/user-macros.xtl" shows the copies.
