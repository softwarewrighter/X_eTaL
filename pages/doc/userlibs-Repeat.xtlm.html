macro libraryuserlibs/Repeat.xtlm

A macro library of your own (userlibs/, found as libraries of your own are): statements written as many times as you ask, when the program is expanded, a loop unrolled at compile time. Import it with an alias of your choice, "r:" u_se< "Repeat", and see what a program becomes with xetal expand FILE.
