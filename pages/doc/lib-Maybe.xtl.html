librarylib/Maybe.xtl

Maybe: a value that may be missing, and the monad that chains computations which can fail (a standard library, built into xetal). Import it with an alias of your choice: "m:" u_se< "Maybe".

A maybe is Church-encoded: it is a function of two arguments, what to give when it is empty and what to do with its value. n_othing gives the first; j_ust x applies the second to x.

Like '+ r_/ A, the function comes first, as a quoted operand, so a chain reads right to left: 'g b_ind 'f b_ind m is f, then g.
