i_nclude< (Rust's include_str!): a file's text while expanding, as a String literal; from disk at the CLI, from the store in the browser; relative to the including file. Errors: missing-file, and a proposed rule for what may be included (no absolute paths, no .. above the project?). Its unused side follows MC22 (one-sided calls): until the user decides, the call shape is a string on each side ("" i_nclude< "data.csv").
